## Patterns worth stealing for CogniKid

- **Morning versus afternoon crash.** Nourify ("fine at 8am, overwhelmed by pickup"), VigorNatura ("come home and crash"), Youth Sport ("hitting a wall at 3pm") and Saphire's teacher ("school drains...") all sell the idea that school drains the tank. That is the same mechanism as our report-card / "mile three" script, which validates the angle.
- **Teacher as a trigger and as proof.** The pain is the note or call home, or "can we have a quick chat?". The payoff is the teacher asking "what changed?" or saying "it's like a different kid". Use both ends.
- **The failed-fixes stack.** Nearly every winner lists omega-3, magnesium, cutting sugar and dyes, reward charts and screen limits before the reveal. It disarms "I've tried everything".
- **Grades as the hard proof point.** "First A in three years" and "teacher says grades are improving" are concrete, dated results the viewer can picture.
- **Meds as the fork in the road.** Most winners position themselves as the option to try before medication, usually voiced by a teacher. Keep CogniKid on the right side of compliance: nutrition support, never "instead of meds".
